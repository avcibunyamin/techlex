Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

TechLex ("we", "our", "the app") is an IT terminology learning application developed by Bünyamin AVCI. This policy explains how we handle your data.

Data We Collect

  • Account information: Email address, username, first name, last name, and date of birth when you create an account.
  • Learning progress: Which terms you have studied, your Leitner box levels, quiz scores, streaks, and XP.
  • Preferences: Language, theme, daily goal, and selected categories.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect location data, contacts, photos, or any sensor data.
  • We do not use advertising trackers or sell your data to third parties.
  • We do not use analytics SDKs that track individual behavior.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the learning experience: syncing progress across devices, calculating streaks and XP, and restoring your account on new devices.

Data Storage

Data is stored on Supabase (PostgreSQL) servers. Your password is handled by Supabase Auth and is never stored in plain text. Learning progress is also cached locally on your device using AsyncStorage.

Data Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete Account. This permanently removes all your data from our servers, including learning progress, profile, and authentication records. This action cannot be undone.

Children's Privacy

TechLex does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us data, please contact us.

Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest revision.
